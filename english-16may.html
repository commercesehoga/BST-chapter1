RC Strategy · Confirmed 2026
How to tackle RC on May 16
3–4 questions per passage are direct (answer is in the passage). 1–2 are inference-based or vocabulary-in-context. Read the questions first, then scan the passage. Watch out for "which of the following cannot be inferred" type — eliminates wrong options. Time: spend max 7–8 minutes per passage.

Subject–Verb Agreement
Appeared on May 15. Expected to appear again.
Rule: "Along with / as well as / together with" → verb agrees with FIRST subject only.
Rule: "Either...or / Neither...nor" → verb agrees with the nearer subject.
Rule: "Each / Every / Anyone / No one" → always takes singular verb.

Idioms & Phrasal Verbs

Idioms appeared in 3–4 Qs on May 14 and 15. Phrasal verbs confirmed in 2–4 Qs per shift. Both likely on May 16.
Bite the bullet
To endure a painful or difficult situation that is unavoidable.
Beat around the bush
To avoid saying what you mean directly; talking about something without getting to the point.
Hit the nail on the head
To describe exactly what is causing a situation or problem; precisely correct.
On the fence
Undecided about something; not committed to either side.
